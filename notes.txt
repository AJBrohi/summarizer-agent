Global Powers Focus on Middle East Energy Security Amid Bab al-Mandab Strait Tensions

RIYADH / GENEVA — October 13, 2026 — Geopolitical friction in the Middle East has intensified this week as international maritime routes faced heightened security risks around the Bab al-Mandab Strait. A major drone attack targeting regional infrastructure over the weekend triggered urgent diplomatic consultations across Washington, European capitals, and Gulf states regarding maritime defense and energy market stability.

Concurrently, delegates gathering in Bangkok ahead of the upcoming international economic summits expressed growing concern over potential supply chain disruptions and global inflation risks. While regional defense alliances have responded with escalated patrol operations, international mediators at the United Nations continue to call for an immediate localized ceasefire and targeted diplomatic engagement to prevent further broader escalation across key commercial shipping lanes.
